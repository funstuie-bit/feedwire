A smaller, clearer library

Based on the live library: 197 feeds, 33 categories. There are no duplicate feed URLs. Repeated publisher names often represent different sections.

  1. Consolidate overlapping categories. Combine UK News + UK News (Neutral to Left); World News + Europe and World News; Tech + Tech News. Keep geography, specialist interests and individual sources available underneath seven compact sections.
  2. Keep subscriptions and reading history. These are navigation changes. Hide the empty Sports category from navigation and keep source lists collapsed until opened.
  3. Give section feeds clear names. BBC Sport → BBC · All sport / F1 / Rugby; CBS Sports Headlines → CBS · MLB / NBA.
  4. One row per article. Match canonical article URLs across feeds, then offer other coverage separately. In a sample of the latest 500 stored items, 54 were extra copies of repeated article links. Grouping different publishers’ coverage of the same event needs a separate, more careful match.
  5. Use the canvas. The inbox fills the available width. Opening a story introduces a compact list and a centred reading column. Focus view removes the list.
  6. Make summaries earn their place. Skip short articles, identify the model, reuse saved summaries, and make the original article the primary view.

This file demonstrates the proposal. The production app and database have not been changed.
